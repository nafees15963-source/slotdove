# Dashboard, deletion and platform administration

Dashboard data lives in the shared server layout. Navigating between sections reuses the workspace instead of loading every table again. Mutations and workspace switching still refresh server data. Dashboard business selection includes owned and joined businesses only; platform access no longer mixes unrelated tenants into the business switcher. Vercel functions are placed in Frankfurt, alongside the Supabase project.

`/admin/login` is a separate platform sign-in entry leading directly to `/admin`. It uses the existing authenticated account and server-checked platform role, without requiring a business. The control center includes businesses, up to 100 accounts, failed booking notifications and the audit log. Booking notification failures do not include invitation-email failures. Email scheduling still requires a configured scheduler and a verified sender for general delivery.

Delete now permanently removes services, staff profiles, invitations and customers. Foreign-key protection prevents deleting records still referenced by appointments. Cancel active appointments first; cancelled, completed and no-show appointments can be permanently deleted, including their history and queued notifications. Deleting a staff profile also removes its tenant membership and linked invitations. Platform admins can delete a whole business after typing its exact name; business data is removed and an audit entry retained. These are user-triggered actions: existing live business data was not deleted during deployment.

Staff cards distinguish scheduling profiles from connected accounts and offer invitations preselected for that profile. A profile alone does not grant account access; invitations must be accepted with the intended verified email.

Shared presentation improvements include mobile marketing navigation, sticky dashboard header, scrollable sidebar on short screens, active-page indicators, pending-navigation feedback, touch-friendly form text, password visibility controls and restrained hover effects. Reduced-motion preferences remain respected.

The hosted security advisor reports the existing intentionally closed private tables and disabled [leaked-password protection](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security#password-strength-and-leaked-password-protection). This update does not change the authentication subscription or enable paid services.

Validation: TypeScript, ESLint, 31 unit tests, 34 isolated PostgreSQL integration checks and production build. Chromium checked the separate admin sign-in, anonymous redirect and responsive width on desktop and mobile. Authenticated live navigation and production latency require verification on the resulting Vercel production deployment; no speed measurement is claimed.
